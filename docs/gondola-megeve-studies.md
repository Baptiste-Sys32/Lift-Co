## V4 shape study: egg-shaped vertical profile

The new `gallery_2313_303_39412.jpg` photo is preserved as `assets/references/gondolas/megeve/jaillet-station-front.jpg` and appears first in the gallery. It drives the current skin proportions; the patent remains a reference for the moving-shell construction.

The cylindrical interpretation was rejected. All three current alternatives have a round footprint at each height, with a smaller base opening into a fuller middle. Above that, an elliptical shoulder sweeps inward over the roof into a small mounting crown. Shell, curved glazing, window gaskets and roof all sample the same radius profile. There is no constant-radius main body with a flat lid. The glazing narrows as it curves over the shoulder.

- **A / Soft egg:** estimated 1.78 m maximum diameter and 2.16 m body height. Base radius is 84% of the middle; the shoulder begins at 72% of the body height.
- **B / High arch:** 1.72 m maximum diameter and 2.24 m height, a slightly smaller base, lower shoulder start and smaller mounting crown.
- **C / Full shoulder:** 1.84 m maximum diameter and 2.12 m height, a fuller base and a later shoulder curve.

The roof mount is narrowed to fit the new crown. Whole-shell opening is retained. Arbois A and Princesse A/C are unchanged by this correction. V3 source, viewer and notes are retained in `archive/backups/megeve-cabins-round-three/`. These remain visual interpretations pending user selection; no browser or automated tests were run.

## Previous drawing-led correction — V3 (superseded)

V3 interpreted the round-body request as a round footprint with upright walls. The user subsequently rejected that cylindrical interpretation and clarified the widening lower body and rounded upper shoulders; V4 above replaces it.

The primary reference found is POMA's [US4149471A patent](https://patents.google.com/patent/US4149471A/en), with a [drawing sheet showing side/end elevations and closed/open plans](../assets/references/gondolas/megeve/schematics/poma-US4149471-drawings.png). Figures 3–4 show curved end shells, a fixed central frame and separate shell pivots. The text describes slightly inclined axes converging upwards. This is relevant construction evidence, not proof of an exact model match to the user's Jaillet photos; no dimensioned factory drawing for those exact cabins was found.

- **A / Round barrel:** a genuinely circular body section, equal 1.78 m estimated width/depth. The shell, curved glazing, roof and floor all use the same circular outline. The fixed central frame occupies side sectors of that circle.
- **B / Twin arcs:** two true semicircular ends with a short straight central section, closer to the patent plan's construction. It is 1.64 × 2.08 m in the study.
- **C / Wide drum:** broader circular ends and a shorter centre, 1.80 × 2.10 m in the study.

All three retain an upright main wall; small changes in radius are confined to the roof and lower skirt edges. The squared superellipse section was removed from the Jaillet geometry. Roof and floor follow the body plan instead of using separate ellipses or domes. Each complete end shell rotates around its own slightly inclined axis. Opening no longer changes the shell scale; the motion remains a visual approximation of the patent arrangement.

The patent sheet is available in the study's **Photos & patent drawing** gallery, with its source link. Use **Roof** to inspect the actual body footprint. The source, viewer and notes from V2 are preserved in `archive/backups/megeve-cabins-round-two/`. Arbois A and Princesse A/C remain selected.

## Reference interpretation

The user's ten images are preserved unchanged under `assets/references/gondolas/megeve/`, with their original visible photographer/site credits. They are the primary visual references. The photographs show historical cabins; searching the same lift names can return different, newer carriers. Do not substitute those automatically.

- **Mont d’Arbois:** tall, nearly vertical glasshouse, clipped corners, dark shallow roof and tapered white tub. A / Lantern is selected; rejected first-round variants are retained in the source backup.
- **Jaillet:** rounded white shell, broad side structure, curved end glazing and an external basket. The user clarified that the shell itself forms the door: the end windscreen, lower painted tub and brow must move together. The study therefore has two large end-shell assemblies that sweep around the fixed central spine, floor and back-to-back benches. They open towards the same side of the cabin, with a small offset between the shells. This is an adjustable visual interpretation of the supplied open-door photos, not a surveyed linkage. There is no static front wall behind a moving shell and no conventional small side door pretending to provide the opening.
- **Princesse:** broad waist, dark belt, narrower roof, inclined end glass and sculpted lower body. A / Panorama and C / Touring are selected. The POMA [January 2003 Princesse press dossier](https://membres.remontees-mecaniques.net/monchu/pdf/dpprincesse.pdf), page A8, identifies the cabin as **ESPACE 8** and describes a door carried on pivoting arms. The study uses a translating side assembly as an approximation, separate from the selected lift's existing station operation.

Additional lookup context: [Megève Tourism — Jaillet](https://www.megeve-tourisme.fr/equipements/telecabine-du-jaillet/), [POMA — historical SP4 reference in the PURE description](https://www.poma.net/les-vehicules-poma/la-cabine-pure/). The latter provides historical context only; the modern PURE geometry is not used. Exact identification of the user's older Jaillet shell is not asserted from the lift name alone.

Dimensions shown in the viewer are visual estimates in metres, not manufacturer specifications. The alternatives share an independent paint control: a colour change does not count as a geometry variant.

## Inspection controls

- Jaillet opens on selected C / Full shoulder; Princesse compares A and C; Arbois shows its selected A. Click an A/B/C card or press A, B or C to inspect one cabin. Escape returns to comparison.
- Compare front, door-side, rear, roof and three-quarter views. In comparison, the models rotate individually for front/side/rear presets to avoid hiding one behind another.
- The Grip preset switches to a single cabin and frames its hanger/clamp details.
- Open the doors or shell with the button or range slider. Roof/hanger removal and glass opacity reveal the interior. Optional seated passengers provide a scale cue.
- Paint can be changed to reference ivory, Lift Co. red, glacier blue or a custom colour. All visible alternatives receive the same colour.
- Reference thumbnails open the full supplied images with previous/next navigation.
- Left-drag orbits, scroll zooms, and right-drag swivels at the camera position.
